How to interpret this index

The overall and factor scores are indices used for charting; the 20 sub-factor values are ordinal ranks. They provide comparative context, not causal evidence. Recommendations in a New Patterns R&D report should remain grounded in that report’s own research evidence.

Source: IMD World Competitiveness Yearbook 2026 booklet, pages 65–68, 66–67, 76–83, and 86–87. This workspace presents extracted factual rankings and does not reproduce booklet pages.
