Doctoral rubric
Ten dimensions, Likert 1-5 (1 = not evidenced … 5 = exemplary) · composite = mean · ≥4.0 exemplary, approve · 3.0-3.9 borderline, review · <3.0 below standard, block. NQF levels are CUMULATIVE: a source claiming Level N must carry Levels 1..N-1 (understand · apply · reflect). The judgment is yours; Sentinari scores, sums, and remembers.
